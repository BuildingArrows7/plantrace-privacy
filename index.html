Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 5, 2026

PlanTrace does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data on its own. Your blueprints, annotations, and measurements never leave your device unless you export or share them yourself.

Data Storage

All data created within PlanTrace — including annotations, measurements, punch items, calibration settings, and voice memos — is stored locally on your device. Annotation data is saved in sidecar files alongside your PDF documents in your local file system.

No Data Collection

PlanTrace does not:

  • Collect any personal information
  • Track your usage or behavior
  • Use analytics, cookies, or tracking technologies
  • Send any data to us or to anyone else automatically
  • Require an account or login
  • Access the internet for any app functionality

Support Requests and Diagnostics

If you choose Help → Send Diagnostics, PlanTrace prepares a short text report and shows it to you before anything is sent. It contains:

  • The PlanTrace version, your device model, operating system version, and language setting
  • Recent PlanTrace activity on your device (for example, which tool was selected and whether a file opened), without file names, folder paths, or drawing content
  • Any PlanTrace crash reports from the last 30 days

The report does not include your drawings, markups, notes, photos, voice memos, or file and project names. It is sent only if you choose to email or share it, and it goes only to the address you send it to. If you email it to us, we use it, along with your email address and anything you write, only to answer your request and fix problems in PlanTrace. We do not sell it or share it with anyone else, and we delete support emails and reports once the issue is resolved.

Device Permissions

PlanTrace may request access to the following device capabilities, which are used solely for in-app features:

  • Microphone — to record voice memo annotations on your blueprints
  • Camera — to capture photos for insertion into your plans (iOS/iPad)
  • Photo Library — to insert existing images onto your blueprints
  • File System — to open, save, and export PDF documents

All data from these permissions stays on your device. No audio, images, or files are uploaded anywhere.

Third-Party Services

PlanTrace does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, or analytics platforms.

Children's Privacy

PlanTrace is a professional construction tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the revised version on this page with an updated effective date.
